# What things weigh

Use these when the photo doesn't tell you. Give a weight with contents included.

## Contents of drawers and shelves (the contents catalog sets the densities)
- Folded clothes: ASTM F2057 fills drawers at 8.5 lb/ft³ (136 kg/m³) for its tests, about a full drawer.
- Books: about 20-25 kg per 80 cm shelf of hardbacks. Paperbacks and children's picture books are lighter.
- Paper files: 20-30 kg in a full letter-size drawer.
- Dishes: a stack of 8 dinner plates is about 4-5 kg.
- Tools: a drawer of hand tools is 10-25 kg.
- Toys in bins: light (2-5 kg a bin) unless they're wooden blocks or books.

## TVs and screens (shape `screen`)
- Flat-screens with their stand: about 0.0045 kg per square inch of diagonal (32 in ~5 kg,
  43 in ~8 kg, 55 in ~14 kg, 65 in ~19 kg, 75 in ~27 kg). The engine computes this from the
  measured width.
- Computer monitors: 3-7 kg. Old CRT TVs: 20 in ~20 kg, 27 in ~35-45 kg, 32 in ~60 kg.

## Things people stand on top of furniture
| Thing | Weight | Depth | Hard / breakable |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Table lamp | 1-4 kg | 15-25 cm | hard; glass or ceramic base breaks |
| Potted plant, 15-20 cm pot with soil | 2-4 kg | 15-25 cm | hard; ceramic pots break |
| Potted plant, 30 cm pot | 8-12 kg | 30 cm | hard |
| Storage box, fabric, with clothes or toys | 1-3 kg | 30-35 cm | soft |
| Storage box, plastic or cardboard, full | 3-8 kg | 30-40 cm | hard |
| Wicker basket with things | 1-3 kg | 25-35 cm | hard-ish (counts as hard) |
| Stack of books | 1 kg per 4-5 books | 20-25 cm | hard |
| Picture frame, glass | 0.5-2 kg | 2-10 cm | breakable |
| Jewelry or keepsake box | 0.5-2 kg | 15-20 cm | hard |
| Plush toys, cushions | under 1 kg | 20-30 cm | soft |
| Baby monitor, speaker | 0.3-2 kg | 10-15 cm | hard |
| Fish tank, small (20 L) with water | 25 kg | 25-30 cm | breakable |
| Microwave | 12-20 kg | 35-45 cm | hard |

A thing is `hard` if it would hurt when it fell on a child's head. Plush, cushions, fabric and
empty baskets are not. `breakable` means glass or ceramic that breaks into sharp pieces.
