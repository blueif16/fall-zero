# Appliances and screens

## TVs
- TVs are involved in most child tip-over deaths in the US (CPSC). A flat-screen standing on a
  dresser or stand is top-heavy on a narrow foot: it falls first, often pulled by a child reaching
  for it or climbing the furniture under it.
- A TV standing on a piece is an item on top, with shape `screen`. Its weight comes from its
  measured width: about 0.0045 kg per square inch of diagonal with its stand (32 in ~5 kg,
  55 in ~14 kg, 65 in ~19 kg). Old CRT TVs are far heavier: 27 in ~35-45 kg.
- A TV on its own floor stand, or on a cart, is judged (`engine: judge`). Carts and stands are tested to UL 1678.
- Fixes: a TV strap to the wall or furniture, a wall mount, or moving it to a low, wide, anchored stand.

## Freestanding ranges and ovens
- 30 in (76 cm) wide, 63-71 cm deep, 91-94 cm to the cooktop, 55-90 kg, steel. The oven door hinges
  at the bottom and opens about 50 cm in front.
- A child standing on the open oven door can tip the range forward, often with hot pots on the
  cooktop. Since 1991 the voluntary standards (UL 858 electric, ANSI Z21.1 gas) require a range to
  stay up with 250 lb on the open door. Ranges meet this with an anti-tip bracket fixed to the floor
  or wall behind a rear foot. Many are installed without it, or the bracket is left off after cleaning.
- Describe it as a case (example `range_30in`): a storage or warming drawer, a bottom-hinged door,
  and a short open band for the cooktop. Material `steel`, `known_mass_kg` from the size.
- Fix: check and install the anti-tip bracket (the strap fix, here a bracket). Ask whether it is
  installed when you can't tell.

## Stacked washer and dryer
- A dryer stacked on a front-load washer without the maker's stacking kit can slide off or tip when
  the washer spins or a child climbs. Judge it. The fix is the stacking kit and a strap.

## Microwaves and appliances on carts or shelves
- A microwave (12-20 kg) on a rolling cart or a high open shelf: judge it. Carts can tip when a
  child pulls the door down. The fix is to move it low or onto a fixed counter.

## Refrigerators
- Rarely tip in normal use: they are deep and heavy. Freestanding narrow units and mini-fridges
  with something heavy on top can. Usually leave them out.
